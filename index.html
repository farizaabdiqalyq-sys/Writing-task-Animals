Theory: Topic Sentences

A topic sentence is usually the first sentence in a paragraph. It introduces the main idea of the paragraph (the central point) and is supported by the other sentences.

Look at three topic sentences about red and grey squirrels in Britain:

  • a At first sight, the two species of squirrel are similar. (General statement)
  • b Three more differences explain why red squirrels have lost out in the competition with grey squirrels. (General statement)
  • c In contrast, the two types of squirrel are different in body size and weight. (General statement + topics of supporting sentences)

The whale shark and the tiger shark

Introduction

The whale shark and the tiger shark are two of the many shark species found in the major oceans of the world. Both the whale shark and the tiger shark belong to the animal class Chondrichthyes. Both types of shark have a large mouth, a divided tail and fins for swimming, including the large triangular fin which can often be seen above the surface of the water. On the other hand, these two types of shark are very different in their physical characteristics, diet, behaviour and conservation status.

Conclusion

Overall, it is clear that the whale shark is a much larger animal, but it is a gentle giant, whereas the smaller tiger shark is a hungry meat-eater. However, both animals play an important role in the ocean environment. For example, tiger sharks keep the oceans clean by eating dead whales and fish. People and nations need to cooperate to make sure we protect both of these important animal species.

Model Body Paragraphs (for reference) Sample

First, whale sharks and tiger sharks differ greatly in their physical characteristics. The whale shark is the largest fish in the sea, growing up to 12 metres long and weighing around 20 tonnes. It has a distinctive pattern of white spots on its dark grey skin. In contrast, the tiger shark is much smaller, usually measuring about 3 to 4 metres and weighing up to 600 kilograms. Its skin is bluish-green with dark vertical stripes that fade as it gets older.

In addition, the two species have very different diets, behaviours, and conservation statuses. Whale sharks are filter feeders that eat tiny plankton and small fish, and they are gentle and harmless to humans. Conversely, tiger sharks are aggressive predators that eat fish, seals, birds, and even rubbish. Finally, whale sharks are currently an endangered species, whereas tiger sharks are classified as near threatened.
